# Checkpoint: Interview preparation

Attempt without notes. Explain mechanisms and draw histories before opening the rubric.

## Scenario

You have one hour to design a scheduler, diagnose a slow request, and explain an unfamiliar frontend race to a reviewer who challenges your assumptions.

## Questions

1. Answer one question from each domain with mechanism, example, failure, trade-off, and validation.
2. Complete a timed mock without reading the rubric, then identify specific gaps.
3. Explain one concept at two depths: two minutes for a teammate and ten minutes with an adversarial history.
4. Revise an answer when new evidence contradicts your first hypothesis.

## Evidence to produce

Record four mock scores and one concrete correction per weak category; repeat after doing the relevant chapter exercises.

Score each answer: 0 = names only; 1 = correct happy path; 2 = failure mechanism and repair; 3 = assumptions, trade-offs, and validation. Pass when every question reaches 2 and the evidence is reproducible. Revisit weak chapters before advancing.

<details><summary>Review rubric after attempting</summary>

Strong communication names assumptions and uncertainty, uses concrete histories, and changes conclusions when evidence changes. Memorized definitions without failure reasoning do not pass.

</details>
